Top Insights (SO WHATs That Matter)

  • Beverages + Home Care deliver 63% of total sales and $1.42M of YoY growth — protect these categories from stockouts.
  • 88.9% in‑stock rate is the biggest revenue leak — Energy Drink 12pk alone represents ~$9.75M annualized lost sales.
  • TPR is the lowest‑ROI promo mechanic (1.24x) — shifting 20% of TPR budget to Display increases ROI 35–40x.
  • OTIF failures are driven by In‑Full (59.8%), not On‑Time (90.3%) — the issue is product availability, not carriers.
  • Root cause 47 (Incorrect Labeling) is fully preventable — eliminating it saves ~$75K annually in fines.
  • Schneider & Central Transport underperform — both require a 90‑day performance improvement plan.
