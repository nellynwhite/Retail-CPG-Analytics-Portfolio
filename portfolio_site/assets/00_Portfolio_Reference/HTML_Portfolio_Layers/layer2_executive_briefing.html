A full year of vendor analytics summarized into four areas: sales performance, inventory risk, promotional effectiveness, and supply chain reliability. Each finding states what happened, what it means, and the recommended action.

Headline findings
Sales Performance +6.8% YoY
What happened
Sales reached $32.23M TY vs. $30.18M LY, a $2.04M gain. Beverages led at +8.0%; Pet grew slowest at +4.0%.
What it means
Growth is volume driven. Selling prices held roughly flat, so the gain came from unit velocity, which is a healthy signal for shelf productivity.
Recommended action
Expand ranging in Beverages. Investigate whether the Pet lag is shelf placement or a velocity ceiling before any de-list decision.
Inventory & In-Stock 88.9% In-Stock
What happened
In-stock averaged 88.9% against a 95% target. An estimated $4.1M in sales was lost to stockouts across 419K units, at 1.4 weeks of supply.
What it means
The 6 point gap is the largest recoverable revenue item in the portfolio. Lost sales equal about 11% of achievable revenue at current velocity.
Recommended action
Reset reorder points on the highest lost-sales store and item pairs. Raise safety stock to a 2.0 weeks-of-supply floor during promo weeks.
Promotional Effectiveness 19.5% avg lift
What happened
128 promo events ran TY on $496K of trade spend, generating 149K incremental units at 19.5% average lift. TPR alone absorbed $296K of that spend.
What it means
TPR returns 0.37 in incremental margin per dollar, below breakeven, while Feature (1.12) and Display (1.00) at least pay for themselves. Trade dollars are concentrated in the least efficient mechanic.
Recommended action
Move trade spend out of standalone TPR and into Feature plus Display combinations, which carry the spend at a higher return.
Supply Chain / OTIF 55.0% OTIF
What happened
OTIF compliance averaged 55.0%. On-Time held at 90.8%, but In-Full was only 60.0%. Total fine exposure was $304K, all supplier controllable.
What it means
OTIF is failing on in-full, not on timing. Orders arrive on schedule but short of quantity, which feeds the same shortages driving the in-stock gap. This is an order-accuracy and availability problem.
Recommended action
Target in-full root causes (product availability, item setup, labeling) rather than carrier timing. Prioritize FreshCo and Peak Snacks, the largest shortage and fine contributors.
