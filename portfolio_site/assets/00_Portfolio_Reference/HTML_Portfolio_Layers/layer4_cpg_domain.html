Recommendation
Trade investment is concentrated in TPR, the only mechanic returning below 1.0. Shifting spend toward Feature and Display raises incremental ROI at the same total budget.
Measurement caveat
Lift is measured against a pre-promo base velocity, excluding the 1 to 2 weeks after a promo. Demand pull-forward depresses those weeks and would overstate lift if not excluded.
Inventory & availability

In-Stock Rate · 88.9%

Share of week, store and item observations with adequate stock. The 6 point gap to Walmart's 95% expectation is the single largest recoverable revenue item, worth $4.1M in lost sales.

Weeks of Supply · 1.4

Forward cover on hand. Below Walmart's 2.0 guidance, which leaves the portfolio exposed to stockouts on any demand spike or supply miss.

Fill Rate · 87.3%

Units sold as a share of units demanded. The gap to 100% is the source of the lost-sales figure. About $370K is recoverable per point of fill rate at current velocity.

Promo Mix · 26.8%

Share of sales during promoted weeks. Within normal range but approaching the level where base velocity erosion becomes a concern, given how much spend sits in TPR.

Walmart OTIF standard
On-Time90.8%
Delivered within the MABD window. Healthy and not the constraint here.
In-Full60.0%
At least 98% of ordered quantity received. This is where deliveries fail. Orders arrive on time but short.
OTIF Compliance55.0%
On-time and in-full on the same PO. A delivery that is on time but 3% short still fails. The combined rate tracks in-full, not timing.
Where the fines come from
Fine exposure of $304K is entirely supplier controllable. The largest preventable causes are product availability, item setup, and incorrect labeling. Carrier timing is not driving the result, so a carrier-focused fix would not move OTIF.
Forecast accuracy

ML forecast · 1.98% MAPE

About 25% more accurate than the naive prior-year baseline (2.65% MAPE). Adopting it upstream reduces the shortage events feeding the in-full gap.

Why this matters

More accurate demand signals mean POs sized closer to true demand, fewer shortfalls, and direct support for the in-stock and OTIF recovery.

Walmart NRF fiscal calendar
Why YoY is reliable here
Walmart's fiscal year ends on the last Friday of January, so Q1 starts in February. Standard Power BI time intelligence aligns to the Gregorian calendar and would shift YoY comparisons by one to two weeks. This portfolio stores TY and LY in the same fact row, pre-aligned by fiscal week, so every comparison holds regardless of calendar drift.
